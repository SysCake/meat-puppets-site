How a bout goes

Every match follows the same script, because the League's lawyers wrote it and nobody dared to change it.

  1. Print

    While matchmaking finds your rival, your puppet is printed live. Meet today's protein.

  2. Warm up

    A few seconds to stretch the legs you were given a minute ago.

  3. THE BOX

    One at a time, each puppet draws a weapon. Both see both. Or you knock the secret knock and bribe it.

  4. Fight

    Anything goes until someone hits zero. Stall, and the arena makes you fight.

  5. KO

    Slow motion, ragdoll, splatter. A vertical replay, ready to share. Time runs out? You both lose.

  6. Recycle

    Loser and winner both get recycled. Unless it was a flawless victory: then the puppet comes home with you.
