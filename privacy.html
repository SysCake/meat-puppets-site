Privacy policy

The puppets are brainless. We still take your data seriously: what the game collects, why, for how long, and how to get rid of it.

The short version

No ads, no trackers

No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps, no advertising identifier. We do not sell data.

No account to create

Online play uses an anonymous player tied to your device. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.

Match records and your stats

The server keeps a record of each round (nickname, weapon, result, counters such as hits, blocks and dodges) to show you your statistics and level, find you fair opponents, balance the game and catch cheating.

Playing against the AI

A match against an AI pilot runs entirely on your device. The game keeps its settings on the device (your nickname, vibration on or off, which tips you have already seen).

At the end of each match the game sends our server a summary of your numbers, so they appear under Your stats and count towards your League level: the AI pilot you faced, the weapon, the result, the length of the fight and counters of what you did (for example attacks, hits, blocks, dodges, damage dealt and taken, distance moved). It is linked to your anonymous player (see Playing online). Without a connection the summary waits on the device and is sent later. Matches against the AI never change how opponents are chosen for you online.

Playing online

When you choose Play online, the game connects to our game server at api.meatpuppets.syscake.it, which runs on Google Cloud in Milan, Italy. Everything travels encrypted (HTTPS and secure WebSockets). This is what the server receives and keeps:

The game engine

The game is built with the Unity engine. When the app starts, the engine may send Unity Technologies basic technical information about the device (such as model, operating system, graphics chip and memory), which Unity uses for its own hardware statistics under the Unity Privacy Policy. We do not receive this information.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no analytics or attribution SDKs, no tracking across other companies' apps and websites. The game never reads the advertising identifier, so it never asks for tracking permission.
  • No sale or rental of personal data, and no profiling for advertising.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. The game only uses the network and, on Android, vibration.
  • No in‑app purchases in the current version. If we add them, payments go through Apple or Google, never through us, and this policy will be updated first.
  • No automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you. The skill rating only chooses opponents in the game; fair‑play warnings are only notes for us to review.

Stores and test builds

The App Store, Google Play and TestFlight are run by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies. If you test the game through TestFlight or a Google Play testing track, the store shares with us what its test programme provides (for example the email address you were invited with, and crash reports you choose to send), and we use it only to run the test.

The Android version offered on this website is downloaded from GitHub (see the next section). Sign‑in with Game Center or Google Play Games is planned; this policy will be updated before it ships.

This website and support

This website has no cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or contact forms, and loads no fonts or videos from other services. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the Android download is served by GitHub: GitHub may process your IP address, browser details, the page or file requested and the time, to deliver them and keep them secure (see GitHub's privacy statement).

If you email us, we receive your address, your message and any attachments. Send only what is needed to help you.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Running online matches (player, nickname, match traffic, connection data): needed to provide the game you asked to play (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Your stats, League level and matchmaking by skill (your statistics, skill rating): needed to provide these game features (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Fair play, security, balancing and bug fixing (match records, technical logs): our legitimate interest in a fair, working game (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object, see your rights.
  • Answering support requests: to handle what you asked (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in keeping a record of it.
  • Legal obligations and disputes, when they apply (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f)).

Who handles the data

Only authorised Syscake staff, and the providers we need to run the service, acting on our instructions: Google Cloud (game server, in the EU), GitHub (website and downloads) and our email provider. Authorities receive data only where the law requires it.

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where a transfer safeguard is required, we rely on an adequacy decision or on standard contractual clauses. Contact us for details.

How long we keep it

Your anonymous player (device identifier, player ID, username, nickname) stays until you ask us to delete it, or until the online service closes. Match records and each round's statistics are kept for up to 24 months; your statistics totals, level, XP and skill rating stay with your anonymous player. Technical logs are overwritten automatically. Support emails are kept as long as needed to handle the request and any follow‑up.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 12 and over. It asks nobody for personal details. If you are a parent and believe your child has sent us personal data, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights and deleting your data

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to your data, a copy of it, correction, deletion or restriction, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Write to meatpuppets-support@syscake.it.

To delete your online data, send us your nickname, your device (for example "Pixel 7, Android 15") and roughly when you last played online. Since there is no account, these details are how we find your player. We delete it, your statistics and your entries in match records, within 30 days and confirm by email. Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device.

You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or to the authority where you live or work.

Changes

The game is still in development. When it changes what it collects, we update this page and its effective date first, and give notice where the law requires it.
