Questions, answered

Getting the game

Where can I get the game?

The game is still in testing. On Android, you can install it today from the Google Play button on this site, which downloads the app directly. On iPhone and iPad it is not out yet: invited testers will get it through TestFlight first. App Store and Google Play listings come later.

How do I install the Android download?
  1. On your Android phone, open this site and tap Get it on Google Play: it downloads MeatPuppets.apk (about 489 MB).
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android asks whether your browser or file manager may install unknown apps: allow it for that app only.
  3. Tap Install, then open the game.

When the game arrives on Google Play, uninstall this version first: the store version is signed differently and will not install over it.

Which devices can run it?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 15 or later; Android phones with Android 7.1 or later and a 64‑bit processor. It is a 3D game: older or low‑end phones may run it less smoothly. Online play needs an internet connection; Wi‑Fi, 4G and 5G all work.

Is it free? Are there ads or purchases?

Free. The current version has no ads and no in‑app purchases.

Playing

I pressed Play online and got an AI pilot. Why?

If no human opponent is found in a short time, the League sends in one of its sentient AI pilots so you are not left waiting. The game always tells you when your opponent is an AI. While the game is in testing there are few players online at once: try with a friend at the same time.

My connection dropped during a match. What happens?

The game reconnects on its own, and you have 15 seconds to get back into the match; your opponent sees that you lost the connection. If you do not come back in time, the round counts as a forfeit.

How do I change my nickname?

From the main menu, in the nickname field. Up to 16 characters: letters, digits, spaces and _ - .. Your opponent sees it, so please do not use your real name.

How do the controls work? Can I use a gamepad?

The Rulebook explains every button. Yes, a Bluetooth gamepad works too.

Problems

The game stutters or feels slow

We are still tuning performance, especially on mid‑range Android phones. Meanwhile: close other apps, turn off battery saver, and let the phone cool down if it is warm. Tell us your device model: it helps us decide what to optimise.

How do I report a bug or a player?

Email us with the details listed at the top of this page. For a player, include their nickname and the date and time of the match.

Your data

What does the game collect?

Very little: no ads, no trackers, no account. For online play, an anonymous player tied to your device, your nickname and a record of each round. Everything is in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device. To delete your online player and match records, email us your nickname, your device and roughly when you last played online; we delete them within 30 days and confirm. Details in the privacy policy.
